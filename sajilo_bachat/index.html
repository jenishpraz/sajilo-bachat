About Us

Built on Member Trust

Sajiloo Bachat Savings & Credit Cooperative Ltd. is a fictional, member-centred cooperative created for this demo. It represents an institution that encourages the habit of saving and invests collected funds in safe, productive and income-generating sectors.

We offer a range of savings, loan and financial services, from business and agricultural credit to education and emergency support, always guided by transparency, accountability and care for our community.

🎯 Our Vision

To be a reliable, transparent and technology-friendly cooperative that builds financially strong and self-reliant members.

🚀 Our Mission

To provide fair-rate savings and credit services that improve the economic and social well-being of every member family.

Our Objectives

  • Offer savings and credit at fair, transparent interest rates
  • Empower women, youth and marginalised groups economically
  • Promote financial literacy and entrepreneurship
  • Support local employment and business development

Our Cooperative Values

Transparency Accountability Honesty Community Development Member Participation
